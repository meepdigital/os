# Ten ethical distributed-compute models

1. Make participation opt-in during installation, with a conspicuous pause/remove control.
2. Show the exact workload, schedule, power estimate, network use, and maximum resource limits.
3. Default to charging only while idle, plugged in, cool, and outside a user-selected quiet window.
4. Let laptops disable compute on battery, metered networks, heat, low storage, or accessibility needs.
5. Return value through hosted storage quotas, backups, identity, private sync, or premium support.
6. Give contributors transparent credits redeemable for services, not mysterious points.
7. Use workloads that benefit society: open-source builds, public research, rendering, or Meep CI.
8. Publish source, job provenance, sandbox policy, and aggregate utilization reports.
9. Prevent crypto mining, surveillance, spam, invasive ad targeting, and undisclosed resale.
10. Make Meep company revenue come from paid organizational hosting, support, managed identity, and ethical compute contracts rather than coercive telemetry.
