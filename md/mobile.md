# Twenty Meep Android integration ideas

1. A Meep Launcher provides a desktop-like app grid, search, workspaces, and widgets.
2. Sync launcher layout and shortcuts with the desktop control plane.
3. Use Meep OIDC for one account across phone and computer.
4. Approve desktop logins with a phone push or passkey.
5. Share clipboard content with explicit per-device consent.
6. Continue browser tabs between Chrome/Firefox-compatible clients.
7. Send URLs, files, screenshots, and text to a selected Meep device.
8. Show desktop notifications on Android with channel-level controls.
9. Use the phone as a webcam, microphone, camera, or presentation remote.
10. Use the computer as a secure display for phone apps through supported Android streaming.
11. Expose a unified contacts and calendar view without copying provider passwords.
12. Sync selected `/home` documents through end-to-end encrypted storage.
13. Let Android trigger desktop scripts through signed, capability-limited commands.
14. Offer a Meep terminal companion with audited SSH and short-lived keys.
15. Pair Bluetooth devices and Wi-Fi credentials through a QR or passkey flow.
16. Show desktop torrent/install/backup progress in the launcher.
17. Use Android biometrics to unlock selected Meep secrets via a hardware-backed flow.
18. Mirror music controls, media queues, and volume between devices.
19. Provide a “find my device” page using user-enabled, privacy-preserving presence.
20. Make every sync category independently selectable, exportable, and revocable.
